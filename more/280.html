Habitat Description

The Tanichthys albonubes is native to the mountain streams of the Guangdong province in China, particularly around White Cloud Mountain. These streams are characterized by clear, cool water with temperatures ranging from 16-22°C (60-72°F). The environment features a moderate flow, with rocky substrates and dense vegetation along the banks, providing both cover and feeding grounds for this species.

Scientific Facts

White Cloud Mountain Minnows belong to the family Cyprinidae and are small, reaching about 4 cm (1.6 inches) in length. Known for their resilience, they can tolerate a wider range of temperatures compared to many other tropical fish. They are currently listed as "Vulnerable" by the IUCN (2024) due to habitat loss and pollution. This species is an egg scatterer, with eggs hatching within 24 to 48 hours, and they exhibit no parental care.

Life Style Summary

These minnows are peaceful and thrive in schools of six or more. They are active swimmers that prefer the middle to upper levels of the water column. In the wild, they feed on small insects, crustaceans, and plant matter. In aquariums, they do well on a varied diet of flake food, micro-pellets, and occasional live or frozen foods like brine shrimp and daphnia. They prefer cooler water and can adapt to both indoor and outdoor setups, including unheated tanks.

Interesting Fact

This species was believed to be extinct in the wild until a small population was rediscovered in the 1980s. It was originally discovered by a Chinese Boy Scout leader, Tan Kam Fei, in the 1930s, which is reflected in the genus name "Tanichthys," meaning "Tan's fish."
