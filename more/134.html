Habitat Description

This species is found in the São Francisco River basin in Brazil, inhabiting clear, fast-flowing streams and rivers with sandy or rocky substrates and dense aquatic vegetation. The water in these environments is typically soft to moderately hard and slightly acidic to neutral, with temperatures ranging from 22°C to 28°C (72°F to 82°F).

Scientific Facts

A member of the Characidae family, this fish is known for its energetic behavior and distinctive silvery-white fin tips. It usually grows to about 5 cm (2 inches) in length. As of 2024, it is listed as Least Concern, although habitat degradation due to deforestation and pollution could pose future risks. These fish are egg scatterers, and their eggs hatch within 24 to 36 hours without parental care.

Life Style Summary

Active and social, these fish thrive in groups of six or more. They are fast swimmers, preferring the middle and upper levels of the water column. In the wild, they feed on small invertebrates, plant matter, and organic debris. In aquariums, they thrive on high-quality flakes, micro-pellets, and occasional live or frozen foods such as daphnia and bloodworms.

Interesting Fact

Males exhibit a striking color change during courtship and territorial disputes. Their body color deepens, and the silver tips of their fins become more pronounced, a display used to establish dominance and attract mates in their natural environment.
