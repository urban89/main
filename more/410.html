Habitat Description

Daisy's Ricefish (Oryzias woworae) is found in the freshwater habitats of Southeast Sulawesi, Indonesia. This species inhabits shallow, slow-moving streams, rice paddies, and small ponds with clear water. The natural environment is characterized by a warm, tropical climate with water temperatures typically ranging from 24-28°C (75-82°F). The habitat often contains dense aquatic vegetation and a soft substrate, which provides hiding spots and breeding grounds.

The conservation status of Daisy's Ricefish is currently not considered endangered, but habitat loss and water pollution pose potential threats to its populations in the wild.

Life Style Summary

Daisy's Ricefish is a peaceful and sociable species that thrives in small groups of six or more. They are active swimmers and prefer the middle to upper levels of the water column. In their natural environment, they feed on small insects, larvae, and other tiny invertebrates. In an aquarium setting, they do well with a diet of high-quality flakes, micro-pellets, and live or frozen foods such as daphnia and brine shrimp.
