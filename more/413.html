Habitat Description

This species is found in the slow-moving rivers, estuaries, and floodplains of Southeast Asia, especially in regions of Indonesia and Thailand. It inhabits both freshwater and brackish environments, favoring areas with dense vegetation, submerged logs, and debris. These fish thrive in water temperatures of 24-28°C (75-82°F).

Scientific Facts

A member of the family Datnioididae, this fish is known for its bold vertical stripes that help it blend with its surroundings. Individuals can grow up to 45 cm (18 inches) long. As of 2024, it is classified as "Vulnerable" by the IUCN Red List due to habitat loss and overfishing. Datnioides microlepis primarily preys on smaller fish and invertebrates.

Life Style Summary

The fish is a solitary predator that hunts by stealth, often staying close to rocks or dense vegetation. It is diurnal, with activity peaks during daylight hours. In captivity, it requires a large, well-maintained tank with plenty of hiding spots and slightly brackish water. The diet should include live or frozen foods like shrimp, small crustaceans, and fish fillets.

Interesting Fact

This species can alter the intensity of its stripes in response to mood, health, or environmental changes. This adaptive coloration is used for communication and enhances its camouflage, making it more effective at hiding from predators or ambushing prey.
