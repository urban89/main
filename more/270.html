Habitat Description

Devario devario is native to the fast-flowing rivers and streams of South Asia, including parts of India, Nepal, and Bangladesh. These waters are clear and well-oxygenated, with rocky substrates and plenty of submerged vegetation. The typical water temperature ranges from 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F), with a slightly acidic to neutral pH.

Scientific Facts

This species belongs to the family Cyprinidae and can grow up to 10 cm (4 inches) in length. Its vibrant blue and gold stripes make it easily recognizable. As of 2024, it is listed as "Least Concern" in terms of conservation, although environmental changes like habitat loss and water pollution could impact its future populations.

Life Style Summary

These fish are social and active, thriving best in groups. In the wild, they feed on small insects, larvae, and plant matter. In aquariums, they do well with a diet of high-quality flakes, pellets, and occasional live or frozen foods such as brine shrimp. They prefer well-planted tanks with open space for swimming.

Interesting Fact

Known for their speed and agility, this species is adept at navigating fast currents in the wild. Their streamlined bodies allow them to swim efficiently through turbulent waters, which provides both exercise and protection from predators.
