Habitat Description

Native to Lake Malawi in East Africa, Fuelleborn's Cichlid inhabits rocky shorelines with strong currents. The waters are hard, alkaline, and clear, providing an ideal environment for these cichlids. The species is typically found grazing on algae growing on submerged rocks and crevices.

Scientific Facts

Belonging to the family Cichlidae, this species can grow up to 15 cm (6 inches) in length. Males are often bright blue, while females show more varied coloration, including orange and brown. Fuelleborn's Cichlid is a maternal mouthbrooder, with females carrying fertilized eggs and fry in their mouths for protection. Currently, their population in the wild remains stable, with no significant threats to the species (2024).

Life Style Summary

These fish are territorial, particularly males, who defend small territories in rocky areas. They feed primarily on algae, which they scrape off rocks using their specially adapted, protruding mouths. In captivity, they thrive on a diet rich in algae-based foods, supplemented with cichlid pellets and occasional protein sources like shrimp.

Interesting Fact

Fuelleborn's Cichlids are known for their distinctive "overbite" due to their specialized jaw structure, which allows them to efficiently scrape algae from rocks, a trait that helps them dominate their niche in Lake Malawi's rocky habitats.
