Paretroplus menarambo is endemic to Madagascar, specifically found in Lake Tseny and its surrounding river systems. This species inhabits shallow, freshwater lakes and slow-moving rivers, with dense vegetation and soft substrates. The water temperature in their habitat typically ranges from 24°C to 28°C (75°F to 82°F), and they thrive in slightly acidic to neutral waters.

Belonging to the family Cichlidae, this cichlid can grow up to 30 cm (12 inches) in length. Paretroplus menarambo is currently listed as "Critically Endangered" due to habitat destruction and overfishing (2024). This species is known for its distinct pinstripe pattern, which provides camouflage among aquatic plants and rocky environments.

Life Style Summary

Pinstripe Damba Cichlids are peaceful and often form small schools. In the wild, they feed on a variety of plant matter, algae, and small invertebrates. In captivity, they require a diet rich in plant-based foods, along with protein supplements. They are substrate spawners and exhibit parental care, with both parents protecting the eggs and fry after hatching.
