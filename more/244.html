Habitat Description

Etroplus canarensis is native to the Western Ghats of India, especially in Karnataka’s river systems. It inhabits slow-moving waters that are slightly brackish, surrounded by dense plant life. The environment provides natural shelter and food, and the water typically has a temperature of 24°C to 28°C (75°F to 82°F) with a pH range of 7.0 to 8.0.

Scientific Facts

This species belongs to the family Cichlidae and can grow up to 10 cm (4 inches). Known for the small, pearl-like spots across its body, it is critically endangered due to habitat loss and its limited geographic range (2024). Conservation efforts are underway to protect its delicate ecosystem.

Life Style Summary

This cichlid is peaceful and thrives in small groups. In the wild, it feeds on algae and tiny invertebrates. In aquariums, it requires a diet that includes algae-based food, supplemented with vegetables and occasional protein. It spawns on flat surfaces and displays strong parental care, with both parents guarding the eggs.

Interesting Fact

A unique behavior of this species is its ability to thrive in slightly brackish environments, an adaptation not common in other cichlids from the region. This allows it to inhabit both fresh and estuarine waters.
