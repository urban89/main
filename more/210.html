Habitat Description

This species is found in the rocky shorelines of Lake Victoria, where the water is clear, and rocks provide numerous hiding spots. It thrives in shallow areas with a depth of 1 to 4 meters, where it can establish territories among the rocks.

Scientific Facts

Part of the Cichlidae family, this fish is known for its vibrant colors and territorial nature. Males can grow up to 10 cm (4 inches) and are especially colorful during breeding. The population is vulnerable due to habitat degradation and competition with invasive species (2024).

Life Style Summary

These cichlids are highly territorial, particularly during breeding. Males defend their rocky territories while females choose mates based on territory quality and coloration. They primarily feed on small invertebrates and algae in the wild and do well on a varied diet of pellets and live food in captivity.

Interesting Fact

This fish exhibits significant color variation within the species, an adaptation to its environment that aids in avoiding predators. Their tendency to interbreed with closely related species has created a complex hybrid population, posing a unique challenge for conservation efforts.
