Habitat Description

The Oryzias latipes is commonly found in the slow-moving streams, ponds, and rice paddies of East Asia, particularly in Japan, China, and Korea. These habitats typically feature clear to slightly turbid water with moderate vegetation. The water is neutral to slightly alkaline, with temperatures ranging from 15-28°C (59-82°F), creating an ideal environment for this resilient species.

Scientific Facts

Medaka are members of the family Adrianichthyidae and usually grow to about 4 cm (1.6 inches) in length. They are known for their genetic diversity and have been extensively studied in scientific research. As of 2024, the species is listed as "Least Concern" by the IUCN due to their wide distribution and stable populations. Medaka are egg layers, and females attach their fertilized eggs to vegetation or substrate, where they hatch in 7 to 10 days depending on temperature.

Life Style Summary

Medaka are hardy and adaptable fish that thrive in groups. They prefer to swim in the middle and upper levels of the water column. In their natural habitat, they feed on small insects, zooplankton, and algae. In aquariums, they are best fed a varied diet of flake food, micro-pellets, and live or frozen foods such as daphnia and mosquito larvae. They can tolerate a wide range of water conditions, making them an excellent choice for beginners.

Interesting Fact

Medaka have been sent to space several times for research purposes due to their genetic similarity to humans in some aspects of DNA repair and development. They were chosen to study the effects of microgravity on vertebrate development and are one of the few fish species to have spawned successfully in space.
