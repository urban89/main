Habitat Description

Etroplus maculatus, commonly found in southern India and Sri Lanka, inhabits both fresh and brackish waters such as lakes, rivers, and estuaries. These environments often feature submerged vegetation and slightly acidic to neutral water, with temperatures ranging between 24°C to 28°C (75°F to 82°F).

Scientific Facts

This species belongs to the family Cichlidae and typically grows to around 8 cm (3 inches) in length. Etroplus maculatus is currently classified as "Least Concern" in terms of conservation status, as its populations are stable across its native range (2024). Its striking orange and yellow coloration, along with distinctive spots, helps it stand out in its natural habitat and the aquarium trade.

Life Style Summary

Orange Chromides are peaceful fish that thrive in groups. In the wild, they feed primarily on algae and small invertebrates, while in aquariums, they require a varied diet including algae wafers, vegetable-based food, and occasional protein-rich supplements. They are substrate spawners, with both parents diligently protecting and caring for their eggs and fry.

Interesting Fact

Unlike many fish, Orange Chromides can tolerate slight salinity, allowing them to thrive in both fresh and brackish environments. This adaptability has made them popular in aquaculture systems where water conditions can vary.
