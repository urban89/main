Habitat Description

The Ceros Tetra (Moenkhausia ceros) is native to the clear, slow-moving waters of the Amazon River Basin in South America. These fish thrive in densely vegetated areas with soft, slightly acidic to neutral water. The natural habitat is typically shaded by the dense canopy of the rainforest, which filters the light and creates a dimly lit environment perfect for these tetras.

Moenkhausia ceros belongs to the family Characidae, which includes a wide variety of small, schooling fish. This species grows to a maximum size of about 6 cm (2.3 inches). The Ceros Tetra is not currently listed as endangered and has a stable population in its natural habitat. Life Style Summary

Ceros Tetras are peaceful and prefer to live in schools of six or more. They are active swimmers, typically found in the middle to upper levels of the aquarium. In the wild, their diet consists of small invertebrates, plant matter, and organic debris. In captivity, they do well on a diet of high-quality flake food, micro-pellets, and occasional live or frozen foods such as brine shrimp and daphnia.
