Habitat Description

The Heros efasciatus, commonly known as Severum, is native to the slow-moving rivers and tributaries of the Amazon basin in South America, particularly in Brazil and Peru. It inhabits areas with dense vegetation, submerged roots, and fallen branches, often in waters with soft, slightly acidic conditions. Typical temperatures range from 24-28°C (75-82°F).

Scientific Facts

Severums belong to the family Cichlidae and are medium-sized cichlids, typically growing up to 20 cm (8 inches) in length. The species is known for its laterally compressed body and variations in coloration, including green, gold, and red morphs. As of 2024, they are classified as "Least Concern" on the IUCN Red List due to their stable population in the wild. Heros efasciatus are substrate spawners, laying eggs on flat surfaces, and they exhibit strong parental care, guarding and fanning the eggs until they hatch.

Life Style Summary

Severums are generally peaceful but can display territorial behavior, especially during breeding. They are omnivorous and feed on a diet that includes insects, small crustaceans, plant material, and detritus. In captivity, they thrive in a well-planted tank with sufficient swimming space and a varied diet of pellets, vegetables, and occasional live or frozen foods like bloodworms or brine shrimp.

Interesting Fact

Severums are known for their intelligence and ability to recognize their owners. They can be trained to take food directly from the hand and will often respond to their owner's presence by swimming to the front of the tank.
