Habitat Description

Found in the Rio Negro basin of Brazil, this species thrives in slow-moving, tannin-stained waters with dense vegetation and soft, acidic conditions. It prefers low-light environments, where submerged roots and leaf litter provide shelter and breeding sites.

Scientific Facts

Part of the Cichlidae family, it is known for its striking vertical stripes and distinct coloration, which can vary widely. Males typically grow to about 10 cm (4 inches). The population is currently stable, though habitat changes could pose a risk in the future (2024).

Life Style Summary

This fish is territorial but generally peaceful, forming monogamous pairs during breeding. It prefers to stay close to the substrate, feeding on small invertebrates and plant matter. In aquariums, it does well with a varied diet that includes pellets and live or frozen foods.

Interesting Fact

This species exhibits unique parental care, with both parents guarding their offspring. It can also alter its coloration based on mood and surroundings, making it a captivating subject for observation.
